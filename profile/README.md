# UpBilim

**An online school platform where students learn, play and make their own games.**

[Website](https://upbilim.com) · [UpBilim Engine](https://github.com/UpBilim/engine)

</div>

---

UpBilim is an education platform for schools, teachers and students. Lessons, classes, homework and exams live in one place, together with games that make learning feel like play. The platform speaks Uzbek, Russian and English.

### What we build

- **Live lessons and classes**: video lessons with a shared board, slides and in-class games; schedules, attendance, homework and exams.
- **Bilimai**: an AI tutor who explains topics, answers questions and helps students practise English conversation.
- **Game Studio**: students describe a game in words, and the AI writes it. The game runs right in the browser, on a computer or a phone, and can be played together on a classroom screen with phones as gamepads.
- **School world**: a shared block world where a school has its own campus, and students explore, build and play.

### Open source

| Project | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| [**UpBilim Engine**](https://github.com/UpBilim/engine) | A tiny game engine for kids' games and AI-written games: 2D, 3D on three.js, physics on Rapier, built-in pixel blocks, animated characters, particle effects and ready-made genre kits. One script, one global `UB`. MIT. |

### Principles

- **Kid-safe by design**: games and AI answers are reviewed for age-appropriate content.
- **Works on weak devices**: the engine lowers quality on its own so games run on low-cost phones.
- **Simple APIs**: easy for a child to read and for a language model to write.
